Fair Play Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short
  • Play with your own knowledge. No tools, no looking answers up, no exploiting bugs.
  • Don't spoil answers, rig duels, or be abusive to other players.
  • Each rule has a number (F1 to F12). Staff quote that number when they take action, so you always know which rule it was about.

Dovedale GeoGuesser is a game of skill. These rules keep the leaderboards meaningful and the community pleasant. They apply everywhere on the Service: solo, the daily challenge, duels, submissions, suggestions and support tickets.

The rules

  • F1 Cheating. Do not use external tools, scripts, automation, saved answer lists, or help from other people or sites to work out a location while playing.
  • F2 Exploiting bugs. Do not take advantage of bugs or unintended behaviour to gain score, time or information. If you find one, report it instead.
  • F3 Score manipulation. Do not tamper with requests, timers or game data, or otherwise try to record a score you did not earn.
  • F4 Boosting and collusion. Do not arrange duels, throw games or trade wins to inflate anyone's score, win rate or streak.
  • F5 Spoiling. Do not reveal answers to players who have not played yet, in particular the daily challenge before it resets.
  • F6 Misusing Suggest Change. The suggestion tools show where locations are. Do not use them to learn answers for play, and do not submit false or harmful corrections.
  • F7 Harassment and abuse. Do not harass, threaten, insult or target other players or staff, on the Service or about it elsewhere.
  • F8 Inappropriate content. Do not use offensive, hateful, sexual or otherwise inappropriate names, labels, hints or messages.
  • F9 Disrupting duels. Do not repeatedly abandon, stall or deliberately disconnect from duels to waste an opponent's time or avoid a loss.
  • F10 False reports and ticket misuse. Do not file reports or tickets you know to be false, or spam the support system.
  • F11 Multiple accounts. Do not use more than one account to gain an advantage: a second go at the daily challenge, extra places on a leaderboard, or a fresh duel rating.
  • F12 Personal information. Do not share, or ask for, another person's personal information anywhere on the Service: real names, addresses, contact details or other accounts.

Account rules A1 to A5 (ban evasion, account sharing, attacking the Service, impersonation, illegal use) are in the Terms of Service. Rules for screenshots, U1 to U14, are in the Upload Guidelines.

What happens if a rule is broken

Staff choose a response that fits how serious the problem is and whether it has happened before:

  • Warning: A recorded notice, with no restriction.
  • Limit: Uploads or duels are switched off for up to 96 hours.
  • Temporary ban: No access to the game for a set time, up to one year.
  • Permanent ban: For serious or repeated violations.

Scores gained by breaking a rule may be removed. Every action is recorded with a reason and the rule number, and you can see it under Moderation History in Settings.

Appeals

If you think an action was a mistake, open a Ban Appeal ticket. You can do this even while banned. A supervisor or operator will review it, and if the appeal succeeds the action is marked as overturned in your history.

Reporting other players

If you believe someone is breaking these rules, open a Report a User ticket. Include the username, when it happened, the rule number if you know it, and any evidence. Please do not confront or name players publicly.

Good sportsmanship

Beyond the rules: take wins and losses well, and help make this a welcoming place for new and experienced players alike.
