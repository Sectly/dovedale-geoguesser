Before you submit a ticket

Support tickets are reviewed by volunteer moderators. Please read before submitting:

check_circleWhat tickets are for: ban appeals, contested submission denials, reporting users or locations, and account issues.
cancelNot for bugs or feature requests - use GitHub instead (link below). Misuse may result in restrictions.
scheduleResponse time: we aim to reply within a few days. We are volunteers and cannot guarantee a specific turnaround.
infoBe specific: include relevant IDs, dates, and links. Vague tickets are harder to resolve.
policyLimit: 3 tickets per day. Do not submit duplicates.
