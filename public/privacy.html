Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short
  • We store your Discord username, display name and avatar, your scores, and what you submit. We never receive your Discord password or email address.
  • We do not sell your data, show adverts, or use tracking cookies.
  • Your scores and leaderboard entries are public. You can make your profile page private.
  • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from Settings at any time.

This policy explains what personal data Dovedale GeoGuesser ("the Service", "we") collects, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and we apply the same rights to all users wherever they live.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Service is run by an individual operator, who is the data controller. You can reach them through a support ticket, or, if you cannot use the ticket system, at sectly.online.

2. What we collect

When you log in with Discord

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. These are refreshed each time you log in.
  • We request only Discord's "identify" permission. We do not receive your email address, password, friends, servers or messages.

When you play

  • Solo scores, daily challenge results (score, guess position, time taken, hints used), duel results and your play streak.
  • Solo games played without logging in are not saved.

When you contribute or contact us

  • Screenshots, labels, hints and map positions you submit, and changes you suggest.
  • Support tickets and replies.

Collected automatically

  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in.
  • A device cookie: a random identifier stored in your browser once you log in. We keep a one-way hash of it against your account. It tells us when two accounts were used in the same browser, and is used only to detect ban evasion.
  • A browser fingerprint: a one-way hash made from technical details of your browser (such as screen size, time zone, language and graphics hardware). It is collected only when you are logged in and is used only to detect ban evasion.
  • Your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit abuse, such as repeated login attempts. We do not store it in our database. Our hosting and network providers may keep standard access logs.
  • A record of logins and of actions taken by staff (the audit log).

Created about you

  • Moderation records: warnings, limits and bans, with the reason and who issued them.
  • Notifications about your own submissions, suggestions, tickets and moderation actions, and the preferences you set in Settings.

4. Ban-evasion checks

When you are logged in we compare your device cookie and browser fingerprint with those of accounts that are currently banned. If they match, your account is flagged for staff to look at. Nothing happens to your account automatically: the Service never bans or limits anyone without a person making that decision.

A match is not proof. Two people who share a computer have the same device cookie, and identical phones, tablets or school laptops can produce the same fingerprint. Staff are told how strong each match is and can dismiss it. If you are banned for evasion and believe that is wrong, open a Ban Appeal ticket: banned accounts can still log in, open tickets, read their moderation history and export their data.

If you delete your account while it has a moderation history, we keep a one-way hash of your Discord ID so that the history, including any active ban, is attached to your account again if you sign up again. See section 8.

5. Who can see your data

  • Everyone: your username, display name, avatar, scores, streak and profile page. Making your profile private in Settings hides your profile page; your name still appears on leaderboards and to duel opponents.
  • Other players in a duel: your name, avatar and guesses for that duel, including to spectators.
  • Staff: moderators, supervisors and operators can see your account details, submissions, tickets and moderation history, and other accounts seen on the same browser. Only operators can export or delete another user's data, and doing so is logged.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Other services involved

  • Discord handles login, and avatars are loaded from Discord's servers. Staff are alerted to new submissions, tickets and moderation actions through a private Discord channel, so usernames, submission labels and short ticket excerpts are sent to Discord for that purpose. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts delivers the icon font. Your browser contacts Google directly when a page loads, which shares your IP address with them. They do not receive anything about your account.
  • Hosting and network providers store and deliver the Service on our behalf.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where data is transferred, it is covered by the safeguards those providers offer, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings. This happens immediately and cannot be undone. It:

  • removes your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar, and replaces your name with "[deleted]" everywhere it appeared;
  • deletes your scores, streak, duel history, tickets, suggestions, notifications, preferences, sessions, device and fingerprint records and any ban-evasion flags;
  • deletes submissions that were pending or denied, and their screenshots.

Two things are kept, no longer linked to your Discord identity:

  • Approved locations you submitted stay in the game. Ask us through the operator contact above if you want one removed.
  • Moderation records stay against the anonymised account, so that the history of how the rules were applied remains intact. With them we keep a one-way hash of your Discord ID, which cannot be turned back into the ID. Its only use is to recognise you if you sign up again, so that deleting an account is not a way out of a ban; your history is then attached to the new account. If you had no moderation history, no hash is kept. We rely on our legitimate interest in the safety of the Service for this. You can object, and we will remove them unless there is a strong reason not to, such as an active ban.

Deleting your account does not delete your Discord account, and we cannot remove data held by Discord.

9. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the Service needs to work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

  • Session cookie: Keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It cannot be read by scripts.
  • Device cookie: A random identifier set once you are logged in, kept for up to a year and not removed when you log out. It is used only for the ban-evasion checks in section 4 and cannot be read by scripts.
  • Login redirect cookie: Remembers which page to return you to after logging in; lasts 10 minutes.
  • Local storage: Remembers choices on your device: map quality, sound on or off, that you dismissed the beta notice, that you accepted the suggestions policy, and which notification you last saw. It never leaves your browser.

10. Your rights

You have these rights over your personal data. Most can be used straight from Settings without asking anyone.

  • Access and portability: Download everything we hold about you as a JSON file, under Your Data in Settings.
  • Erasure: Delete your account in Settings.
  • Rectification: Your name and avatar come from Discord; change them there and log in again. For anything else that is wrong, open a ticket.
  • Restriction: Make your profile private in Settings, or ask us to pause use of your data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Objection: You can object to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interest, including leaderboards, fingerprinting and kept moderation records. We will stop unless we have a compelling reason, such as an active ban.
  • No automated decisions: Bans and limits are always decided by a person, and you can appeal any of them.

To use a right that is not available in Settings, open a support ticket, or contact the operator at sectly.online if you cannot log in. We reply within one month. We may need to confirm you own the account first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EU, the authority for your country.

11. Children

The Service is not aimed at children under 13, and you need a Discord account, which has its own minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Connections to the Service are encrypted, login is handled by Discord so we never see your password, and access to user data is limited to staff who need it, with their actions logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your data and put you at risk, we would tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes, and the date at the top shows the latest revision. Where a change significantly affects how your data is used, we will aim to announce it on the site before it takes effect.
