Upload Guidelines

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short
  • Submit clear screenshots from inside Dovedale Railway, with the pin where the picture was taken.
  • No player names, chat or game menus in the picture, and no edited or AI-made images.
  • If a submission is denied, the reason will quote one of the numbered rules below (U1 to U14).

These guidelines cover new location submissions and suggested changes to existing ones. They keep Dovedale GeoGuesser fair, varied and enjoyable. Submissions that break them are denied; repeated problems can lead to an upload limit.

check_circle What makes a good submission

  • Taken inside the Dovedale Railway Roblox game world.
  • Up to date: it shows the place as it looks in the game today.
  • Identifiable: landmarks, track features or structures give players something to reason from.
  • Clear and well lit, not blurry, heavily compressed or hidden by fog and effects.
  • Reasonably difficult: not instantly obvious, not impossible.
  • An accurate pin, placed where the screenshot was actually taken.
  • A short label naming the rough area, which helps staff verify the pin.

cancel The rules

A submission or suggestion will be denied if it breaks any of these. Staff quote the number in the denial reason.

  • U1 Wrong game. The screenshot is not from the Dovedale Railway game world.
  • U2 Player names visible. A player's username appears anywhere in the picture: nametags, overhead labels, leaderboards or chat. NPC names are fine.
  • U3 Chat or interface visible. Chat messages, menus, HUD elements or other overlays are in the picture. The one exception is Roblox's own top bar (the menu, chat and inventory buttons), which some devices cannot hide. Crop it out where you can do so without breaking another rule.
  • U4 Duplicate. The same view, or nearly the same view, is already in the game.
  • U5 Wrong pin. The pin is not where the screenshot was taken, whether by mistake or on purpose.
  • U6 Poor quality. The image is too small, blurry, dark or compressed to be usable.
  • U7 Not identifiable. Nothing distinctive is in frame, for example a close-up of a wall, the inside of a vehicle, or open sky.
  • U8 Filters or edits. Freecam filters, visual effects, or any editing of the picture. Cropping and resizing are the only changes allowed.
  • U9 Inappropriate content. Offensive, sexual, hateful or unrelated content, in the picture or in its label and hints.
  • U10 Misleading label or hints. The label or hints are wrong, give the answer away outright, or are not about the location.
  • U11 Spam. Joke entries, or many low-effort submissions sent to flood the review queue.
  • U12 Not yours to share. A screenshot taken by someone else without their permission, or one showing another person's personal information.
  • U13 AI-generated or AI-altered. An image made, extended, upscaled or otherwise changed by an AI tool. Submit the screenshot exactly as it looked in the game.
  • U14 Out of date. A screenshot from an older version of Dovedale Railway that no longer matches how the place looks in the game now.

Tips for a great screenshot

  • Use a private server: It keeps the area clear of other players.
  • Use freecam (Shift + P) to position the camera. First-person view also works as long as the game's own interface is hidden.
  • Hide the interface and overhead names: Freecam can switch both off, which is the easiest way.
  • Players and trains may appear but are not preferred: they distract and date the image.
  • Day and night are both accepted; daytime and clear weather are preferred for visibility.
  • Include enough background to give context without making the answer obvious.

Suggesting changes

From the Submit page you can suggest a new screenshot, a corrected pin, a hint, a full edit, or the removal of an existing location. The same rules apply. Because this tool shows where locations are, it is only open to accounts at least 7 days old that have played at least five games, and using it to learn answers breaks Fair Play rule F6.

What happens to your submission

  • Staff review it and either approve or deny it. You get a notification either way, and can follow it under My Submissions in Settings.
  • Staff may adjust the label or hints when approving.
  • If it is denied, the screenshot is deleted straight away and the record is removed after 30 days.
  • Approved screenshots become part of Dovedale GeoGuesser. See the Terms of Service for the permission you give us and how to ask for removal.

Appeals

If you think a submission was wrongly denied, open a Submission Appeal ticket and mention the submission number.
